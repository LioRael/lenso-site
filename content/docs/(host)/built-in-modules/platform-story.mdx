`platform-story` powers Lenso Console stories. It is included by the platform
composition and should not be installed like an application module.

It owns:

- `/admin/runtime/stories` and story detail routes.
- Runtime story display metadata for HTTP routes, business operation calls, remote proxy
  calls, functions, and background work.
- The Stories Console surface under the configured Console Service URL.

Use it to inspect what happened across a request or workflow. Do not put
business data or feature-specific UI in `platform-story`; author a Linked Module
or an independently running Service for that.
