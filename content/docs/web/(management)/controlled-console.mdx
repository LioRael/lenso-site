This candidate tutorial awaits the ordinary Management source App receipt and
browser journey. The intended result is one approved business update with an
auditable domain receipt, followed by a working business App with Console
disabled. A rendered page or an owner-only PostgreSQL test does not complete
this journey.

## Prepare the source App and its proofs

From the selected [Lenso Examples](https://github.com/LioRael/lenso-examples)
checkout's `fixtures/ops-reference`, build the exact CLI with
`python3 prepare.py --cache candidate-tools`. Use the executable recorded in
`candidate-tools/tools.json` and authorized private test-database URI files:

```sh
python3 verify_management.py --cli /absolute/path/from/tools.json/lenso \
  --database-url-file /private/path/test-postgres-uri \
  --audit-url-file /private/path/test-audit-postgres-uri \
  --receipt management-receipt.json
```

This candidate assembly uses ordinary CLI discovery and saved named dependency
choices. Bootstrap is removed before the final build. Its verifier deletes the
source before startup, then checks admission, approval, business receipts,
audit attribution and restart. It preserves the owned directory for inspection;
do not repeat bootstrap after an uncertain response.

Run the [operators authentication journey](/docs/web/operators-auth) with its
browser-ready options to obtain a real human Console receipt. Use distinct
browser contexts to request and approve the same immutable intent, then inspect
the committed result. PAT secrets must disappear after hide or unmount and
must not enter persistent browser storage.

Installation removal uses the same completed API, human and real delegated
Agent receipts:

```sh
python3 verify_installations.py --cli /absolute/path/from/tools.json/lenso \
  --api-root /private/path/completed-api-profile \
  --human-root /private/path/completed-human-profile \
  --api-receipt management-receipt.json --human-receipt human-receipt.json \
  --agent-receipt agent-receipt.json --receipt installations-receipt.json
```

The installation verifier rejects a fake transport credential as Agent proof.
It checks selected compositions and removal without changing the business
state. These candidate commands do not claim completed qualification; retained
source App, browser and joint Agent receipts are required for publication.

## Select the optional surface

The existing [App Console](/docs/core/app-console) guide describes the separately
verified development kit and contribution compiler. Its service allow/deny
example is an illustrative business rule; adding a page does not supply
production identity authentication or management permission.

In the candidate assembly, the business App runs without Console and without
an Agent. Console projects owner-published management entries and approved
operations; it does not own business tables, login state, grants or decisions.
Selecting the Web surface cannot change a private backend selection.

## Review an immutable operation

The guard must perform these checks for the selected deployment and entry:

1. Verify an operators assertion and current CredentialState.
2. Intersect the credential ceiling, current RBAC and the Host's admitted
   entry policy, including target resource scope.
3. Publish the exact versioned input schema and create immutable write intent
   with the operation ID and expected resource revision.
4. Have Approval decide that exact intent through a currently admitted human
   route. A self-supplied caller, approver or assurance value grants nothing.
5. Recheck authority at invocation, call the business owner's generated port
   and retain the domain receipt plus attributed management audit events.

A target, schema, parameters, scope or revision change invalidates the approved
intent. Cancelled, denied and expired decisions do not execute it. Complex
domain pages remain authored contributions; a catalog does not generate a full
business UI.

## Recover an unknown invocation

An interrupted response after dispatch can leave the result `unknown`.
Console must preserve the invocation identity, show its status and request
receipt reconciliation from the same business owner. It must not label the
operation failed or submit it again automatically. The durable domain receipt
and Audit provenance are different facts; neither is fabricated by the page.

The Console owner has Native PG authority fixtures and the management surface
has browser checks. Those prove their recorded boundaries. Exact clean source
App setup, independent realm admission, approved write, attribution,
reconciliation and Console-disabled behavior remain the promotion gate for
this tutorial; Workers management remains unassessed.

Use the owner's explicit shutdown and test-resource cleanup after saving
receipts. Disabling Console removes the optional experience, not the business
Plugin, its state or its independent authorization rules.
